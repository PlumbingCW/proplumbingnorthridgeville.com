Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026.

☎ Call (440) 468-0237

What we collect

This website has no forms and no accounts. If you call, we collect what you tell us so a plumber can help, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem. If you email contact@proplumbingnorthridgeville.com, we receive your email address and your message.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Tell the dispatcher if you have a concern about this.

Website data

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. Pages on this site load fonts and the map from our own files and from Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when the map loads.

How we use information

We use it to answer your request, schedule and perform plumbing work, and keep records of that work. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only when needed to do the job, to follow the law, or to protect our business, for example with a permit office when your job requires a permit.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it, email contact@proplumbingnorthridgeville.com or call (440) 468-0237. We will respond as the law requires.
