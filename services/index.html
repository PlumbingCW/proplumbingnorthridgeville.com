Emergency and repair

Services for problems that cannot wait

Leaks, backups and equipment failures. The line is open every hour of every day, so call (440) 468-0237 any time.

Drain Cleaning

Cables, cameras and jetting for slow, stopped and repeating clogs.

Water Heater Repair

Leaks, dead elements, pilot trouble and error codes on electric, gas and hybrid tanks.

Water Heater Replacement

Tank swaps for failed gas, electric and heat pump water heaters, with the old unit hauled out.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water that scours grease, scale and roots from the full pipe wall.

Leak Detection

Meter checks, acoustic listening, thermal imaging and cameras to find hidden water leaks.

Sewer Line Repair

Camera diagnosis, spot repair, trenchless lining and full replacement for broken sewer lines.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Rodding, jetting and camera work for the main line from house to street.

Commercial Plumbing

Drains, lift pumps, grease traps and leaks in shops, offices and restaurants.

Toilet Repair

Running toilets, leaks at the base, wax rings, flanges and full replacement.

Faucet & Sink Repair

Dripping faucets, broken shutoff valves, shower valves and tub drains.

Garbage Disposal Repair

Jammed, leaking and dead disposals, plus installation and dishwasher hookups.

Burst Pipe Repair

Cracked, split and frozen pipe cut out and replaced, with a clear price first.

Water Line Repair

Buried service line leaks found, dug to and repaired or replaced.

Sump Pump Repair

Sump and sewage ejector pumps repaired, replaced and installed.

Planned work

Installs, inspections and upgrades

Work you can schedule ahead. Ask the plumber about permits when you book, or call to set a time.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs with permit, venting and expansion tank handled.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless service, descaling flushes and installation for gas and electric units.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Video of the line from your house to the street, with the problem located.

Repiping

New supply pipes inside the house when patching no longer works.

Plumbing Installation

New fixtures, bathrooms, gas lines and new-build plumbing.

Basement Waterproofing

Sump pumps, interior drains and backup protection for wet basements.

Boiler Repair

Water-side boiler repair for homes and small commercial buildings.

Water Softener & Filtration

Softeners, conditioners and whole home filters, installed and serviced.
